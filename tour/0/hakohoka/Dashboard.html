X-Axis: Difficulty bins in steps of 5 bounded by their lower/upper limits

Y-Axis: Vintage bins in steps of 5 years< bounded by their lower/upper limits

Color: Mean of correct guessers across songs in this vintage-difficulty bin

Example:

  • Vintage: 2015-2020
  • Difficulty: 25-30
  • Mean Over-8: 4.26

Means that on average, roughly half of the room got songs from 2015-2020 with difficulty of 25-30 correct

Y-Axis: Median vintage across songs this player guessed correctly

Size (Guess Rate)

Color: Calculates this player's value (Usefulness) against what's expected from their Elo

Example:
A small, blue circle in the bottom-left means that, on average, this player:

  • Has low Guess Rate (small),
  • Is over-performing their Elo (blue), and
  • Usually hits hard (left), older (bottom) songs

X-Axis: Mean of correct guessers across songs from this player's list

Y-Axis: Median vintage across songs from this player's list

Size (Rig Rate)

Example:
A big, red circle in the top-right means that, on average, this player's list:

  • Usually has newer (top) songs,
  • Appears a lot (big),
  • Is difficult for the player (red), yet
  • Easy for others (right)

Advanced Column Filters Guide ⚠︎

Filter songs using columnName:value or matching arithmetic operators (<, >, <=, >=). Wrap multi-word properties in double-quotes.

String Columns japanese, english, song, artist, composer, arranger, tags, genres, animetype Example: guessers>2 japanese:aikatsu or japanese:"91 Days" songtype:op
Numeric & Rank Columns difficulty, vintage, rank, score, guessers, listers Difficulty floor: difficulty:14 (14.xx)
Math operators: difficulty<=14 (≤ 14.00)
Rank (inverted): rank>492 (Top 491 or better)
Username check: guessers:HakoHoka
Vintage Chronology Specific season/year matching: vintage:2023 or vintage:summer2021
Direct Timeline sorting: vintage<"Fall 2008"
Song Type & Binary Type (ignores numbers): songtype:ed or songtype:insert
Booleans (yes/no/true/false): rebroadcast:false or dub:yes

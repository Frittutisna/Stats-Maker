Guide

Search using value or columnname:value

You can replace : with arithmetic operators (=, !:, !=, <, >, <=, >=),
uf with usefulness, guess rate with gr, 1/8s with solos, 2/8s with doubles, and 7/8s with sevens

Combine query terms using explicit and/or keywords
Group precedence with (brackets)
Wrap multi-word values in "double-quotes"

elo<90 or (opgr>80 edguessrate<30) returns players that either have an Elo below 90 or those who have OP and ED Guess Rates above 80% and below 30%

X-Axis (Difficulty): Difficulty bins in steps of 5 bounded by their lower/upper limits

Y-Axis (Vintage): Vintage bins in steps of 5 years< bounded by their lower/upper limits

Color (Over-8): Mean of correct guessers across songs in this vintage-difficulty bin

Example
  • Difficulty: 25-30
  • Vintage: 2015-2020
  • Over-8: 6.26

Means that on average, 6.26/8 people guessed songs from 2015-2020 with difficulty of 25-30 correctly

X-Axis (Over-8): Mean of correct guessers across songs this player guessed correctly

Y-Axis (Vintage): Median vintage across songs this player guessed correctly

Size (Guess Rate)

Color (Score): Calculates this player's value (Usefulness) against what's expected from their Elo

X-Axis (Over-8): Mean of correct guessers across songs from this player's list

Y-Axis (Vintage): Median vintage across songs from this player's list

Size (Rig Rate)

Color (Rig Guess Rate)

Example

A small, blue circle in the bottom-left means that, on average, this player:

  • Has low Guess Rate (small), yet
  • Is over-performing their Elo (blue),
  • Usually hits harder (left) songs, and
  • Prefers the older (bottom) ones

Example

A big, red circle in the top-right means that, on average, this player's list:

  • Usually has newer (top) songs,
  • Appears a lot (big),
  • Is difficult for the player (red), yet
  • Easy for others (right)

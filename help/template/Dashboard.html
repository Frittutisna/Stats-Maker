Guide

Search using value or columnname:value

You can replace : with arithmetic operators (=, !:, !=, <, >, <=, >=),
uf with usefulness, guess rate with gr, 1/8s with solos, 2/8s with doubles, and 7/8s with sevens

Combine query terms using explicit and/or keywords
Group precedence with (brackets)
Wrap multi-word values in "double-quotes"

elo<90 or (opgr>80 edguessrate<30) returns players that either have an Elo below 90 or those who have OP and ED Guess Rates above 80% and below 30%

%/Δ
Swaps base percentages with their respective delta (Δ) variants

Guide

Click the ⚙ button to configure your view settings
Only one chart is visible at any time

Sort:
• Global: Sorts and ranks all players globaller
• Tier: Sorts and ranks players within their respective Tiers

Display:
• Rate: Shows percentages
• Count: Shows raw counts

Chart:
• General: Charts various metrics (Correct, Over-8, (Off) Rig, Chanting)
• Contribution: Charts direct contribution to life count (Lives Taken/Saved, Other Correct)
• Time: Charts Time aggregate metrics (Minimum, Mean, Median, Maximum, or Standard Deviation)
